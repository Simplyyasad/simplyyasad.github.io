A little about me

Creative thinking. Precise execution.

I'm an independent video editor and creative visual designer. I help creators and brands turn raw footage and rough ideas into polished visuals that hold attention and feel considered, frame by frame.

My work spans fast, scroll-stopping short-form edits for Instagram and other platforms, cinematic storytelling with deliberate pacing and sound, motion graphics that add clarity and style, and colour grading that gives every project a cohesive look. I also create AI-assisted advertising visuals for brands that want fresh promotional ideas.
